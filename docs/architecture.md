**Current checkpoint:** public `Maestro4567` / 1.1.5 at `236669af71e0dd1a1b6064645c91dd2c6b2e0625`. For exact source, inspect `src/main/java/com/maestroai/ai/`, `src/main/java/com/maestroai/training/` and `verification/` at the chosen commit.

## Live authority

`MaestroBattleAI.choose` receives the live native choice. `LivePromotionGate` and the owned request/turn/actor leases require valid current source, independent legal action roots and final legality checks. `ShadowLiveDecisionEngine` ranks actions using a first-party, source-constrained policy; `ShadowNativeLegalWindow`, `ShadowPromotionPreparation` and related native-own transport/legality mechanisms permit supported moves, setup, recovery, status, switching, forced replacements and valid gimmicks. Shadow does **not** borrow Legacy's tactical move ranking. The owner-authorized live primary is **Shadow**; **Legacy is an emergency fallback**, not normal decision dependence.

A successful code build cannot prove that every native branch or installed scenario actually selects Shadow. The owner's earlier 1.1.3 Minecraft log had 23 Shadow choices and six Legacy fallbacks; v1.1.4–1.1.5 source and regressions addressed those issues, but installed 1.1.5 acceptance remains unverified.

## Public opponent observations and uncertainty

`PublicLiveOpponentSnapshot` and the source-leased public-history pipeline accept only admitted opponent-visible events: displayed appearance, public HP, revealed moves/items/abilities, switching and source-backed form/type/Tera signals. Ordinary display can be a Zoroark Illusion: lookup of the **displayed** species's public types is a conditional tactical hypothesis, **not** a confirmed species identity or hard immunity guarantee. Explicit native public revelations can justify stronger facts only under verified continuity. Transform, unverified forms, stale history and malformed private/public mixing fail closed. Never read or infer hidden opponent team, held items, unrevealed moves or private requests.
