# Phase 6.2 — Offline tactical provenance gates

**Scope:** Strict offline, deterministic synthetic public-fixture validation; not native runtime evidence or tactical authorization. Phase 6.1 exact-commit Actions #40360852732 was still pending when this work began; successful CI is not assumed.

`OfflineShadowProvenanceGate.check` verifies an externally fixed SHA-256 fixture digest over length-prefixed original framed public history, pinned turn, side slots, opponent appearance ordinal, and declared own legal actions. Hashes detect modifications to independently fixed fixtures; they are **not** signatures, independent source authentication or trusted custody. Caller-minted pins and input records never confer authority.

The gate re-runs `PublicProtocolFilter`, `PublicEnvelopeProvenance`, `PublicProtocolEvidence`, and `PublicReplyCoverage.currentReplies`. Its narrow protocol slice accepts only unambiguous direct spectator `switch`, `drag`, `turn`, and `move` events. It rejects private sideupdates, split packets, requests, unsupported mechanics events, actor/side mismatches, stale/nonmonotonic turn, duplicate/reused appearance identity, Transform/Illusion without reconcilable public basis, unseen or cross-appearance replies, HP inconsistency and any opponent types/boosts/hidden reserves claimed without a separate public evidence contract. Rejected inputs never expose raw player names, replay bytes or secrets in exceptions.

Caller-listed own legal actions must exactly match the offline snapshot; forced/trapped legality remains subject to Phase 6.1's original `PublicStateSearchAdapter` validation. No subsequent own decision is admissible without a separately validated own actor/turn receipt. Phase 5.21 graded possibilities do not enter the legal-action or response set. Phase 4 source-conditional bounds are preserved without falsely certifying installed mods or opponent unknown effects.

`OfflineShadowTacticalAdapter.inspectWithProvenance` performs the new check **before** its Phase 6.1 conditional assessment. Outputs have private-constructor, redacted offline receipts, immutable collections, and unconditional denials for source authentication, runtime binding, actual legal-action certification, unique opponent identity, calibrated probabilities, Shadow tactic readiness and live promotion. These contracts are for FrAIL's independent inspection; they do not modify `MaestroBattleAI` or `LivePromotionGate`.

**Adversarial synthetic regression:** frozen-digest tampering, copied/mutated messages, concealed requests/split data, current/stale turn, wrong actor and appearance, same-nickname return, unseen reply, unsupported Transform/Illusion, unobserved opponent typing/HP, injected legal moves, and forged future requests. Phase 6.1 regression remains integrated in the same standalone runner. No real replay intake, Minecraft test or main merge was done.

**Public CI scheduling:** This isolated development branch cancels obsolete running same-branch verification when newer commits arrive. Required build, standalone regression, JAR and source-oracle steps are unchanged. This is not a green-CI assertion.
