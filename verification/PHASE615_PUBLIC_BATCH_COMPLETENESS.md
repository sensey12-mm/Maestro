# Phase 6.15 — Multi-event public-envelope completeness and private-route isolation

**Branch:** `Maestro-6` · **Version:** `1.0.60` · **Feature:** existing opt-in public observation and 6.13 read-only consistency · **Authority:** none.

## Source-level investigation

The pinned Cobblemon Showdown history is read from the same `PokemonBattle.getShowdownMessages()` observation point as Phases 6.9–6.14. Inspection of `PublicProtocolFilter`, `PublicEnvelopeProvenance` and `PublicProtocolEvidence` establishes that a Showdown `|split|p1` / `|split|p2` routes its **first payload as private and second as public**. The previous public filter can extract the second, but an extracted value is not an authenticated replay/delivery receipt. Consequently **split packets are not admitted to the Phase 6.15 readiness stream**. The test suite independently verifies the split provenance parser's public/first-private ordering while requiring the 6.15 source to abstain for a complete, partial, nested, unsupported or mixed split. Untrusted `sideupdate`, requests and PP updates remain rejected. No private first-half bytes are copied into accepted source frames.

## Implementation

`PublicBatchEnvelopeGuard` now parses the **entire original bounded direct `update` chunk**, 1–32 events per chunk and at most 512 events across 128 chunks; no ignored trailing event, malformed framing, nested update, carriage return, unknown event, unsupported annotation, empty frame, split or private sideupdate can be reinterpreted as a complete packet. It recognizes a constrained subset of non-state annotations `|-crit|`, `|-supereffective|`, `|-resisted|`, `|-immune|`, `|-miss|`, `|-fail|`, `|-notarget|`, `|-nothing` and `|upkeep`. Actor-bearing annotations require the currently displayed actor for that public slot and a move in that turn; `upkeep` requires an observed turn. These annotations never become legal actions, damage evidence, predicted outcomes or source-authenticated identities.

For each complete original history, the batch guard creates an **internal mechanics-only sequence**; Phase 6.14's existing source-pinned parser validates that sequence for full turn, active appearance, HP/status, faint, switching, Illusion and transform continuity. The bridge also independently extracts the direct public mechanics via `PublicProtocolFilter.publicEvents` and checks `PublicEnvelopeProvenance.reconcile` for strictly `DIRECT_UPDATE` origin on every mechanical event. **Any disagreement rejects the complete observation**, rather than dropping the unknown event and silently asserting completeness. The bridge retains only validated direct-public update chunks and their digests, never private routes.

`publicEventCount` now counts all validated public mechanics **plus** approved annotations, while `PublicSourceLease` checks the original chunk count and public-event count separately. Any new chunk expires previous leases, even if it is an annotation-only suffix. Histories are append-only and rewriting an accepted chunk permanently withdraws the bridge. A complete multi-event public source may provide a 6.13 `CONSISTENT_READ_ONLY` report when an independent current native own-action witness also matches, **but this reports structural consistency only**, not actor custody, complete installed legality, opponent responses or tactical capability.

**Important old-contract guard:** Phase 6.7/6.8 `probeSynthetic` still accepts only the **original single-event narrow `switch/drag/turn/move` frames**. Any multi-event batch or annotation disables this synthetic fixture path, even if it is a valid public observation. Illusion `replace`, `detailschange`, `-formechange` or `-transform` still makes the entire public appearance ambiguous and blocks 6.13 public-source leases.

## Adversarial regression acceptance

`PublicBatchEnvelopeGuardRegression` is registered with `StandaloneRegression`. It covers multiple mechanics plus annotations inside direct batches, repeated polling, event-count/source revision continuity, next-turn miss/fail, annotations without prior move, unsupported/private trailing events, invalid status/HP/turn/actor fields, trailing partial frame, nested `sideupdate`, split second public-half extraction but **no bridge promotion**, incomplete and unsupported split, mixed annotations/split, carriage returns, rewrites of previously accepted batches, and Illusion in an otherwise valid multi-event batch. All Phase 6.1–6.14 test suites remain registered.

Supported annotations are **deliberately narrower than the full Showdown protocol**; many battle mechanisms, full public source capture across native dispatch, multi-line instructions with unrecognized tags, and secure split-delivery attestation remain out of scope. Fail-closed abstention is expected in many real battles. Do not claim in-game runtime compatibility or complete public-event coverage until real Minecraft testing and independent review occur.
